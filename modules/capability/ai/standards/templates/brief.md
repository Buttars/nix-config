One page. An agent that reads this should be oriented without exploring.
Describe systems, never people or their records.

## What this is

Two or three sentences. What the domain covers, where its boundary is, and who
owns it.

## Current state

What is true today. Deployed versions, active migrations, known-broken things.
Date anything that will age.

## Decisions in force

the full entry in `decisions.md`. A summary, not the log.

## Open questions

discussed" from "discussed and deferred".
