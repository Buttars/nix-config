# ADR <n>: <title>

A decision record for a choice significant enough to stand alone — one that
crosses team boundaries, is expensive to reverse, or will be asked about by
people who were not in the room. Smaller decisions belong in the domain's
`decisions.md`.

**Status** — proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR <n>
**Date** — <YYYY-MM-DD>
**Deciders** — <who signed off>

## Context

The forces in play: the constraint, the problem, the pressures that make this a
decision rather than a default. Enough that a reader a year from now understands
why it was hard. State what was true at the time, not what is true now.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Alternatives

Each option seriously considered, with what ruled it out. Include the option of
doing nothing. An ADR whose alternatives are obviously bad is an announcement,
not a record.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what this forecloses. Name the
costs accepted, not just the benefits gained — the costs are what a future
reader needs to weigh when reconsidering.

## Revisit when

The condition that would make this worth reopening. If no such condition exists,
say so.
