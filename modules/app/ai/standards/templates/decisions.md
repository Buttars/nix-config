# <domain> — decisions

Append-only. Newest last. Never rewrite an entry — the history is the value.

To reverse a decision, append a new entry that supersedes it and add a
`Superseded by` line to the old one. That is the only edit permitted to an
existing entry.

---

## <YYYY-MM-DD> <short title>

**Decided** — what was chosen, in one or two sentences.

**Why** — the reasoning that made this the choice. Not a restatement of the
decision.

**Rejected** — the alternatives considered and what ruled each out. An entry
with no rejected alternatives usually means no decision was made.

**Consequences** — what this commits us to, and what it costs.

**Status** — in force | superseded by <entry>
